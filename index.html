    Restraint by design

    Built to Do One Thing Extremely Well.

    CIP investigates alerts. These are the things it deliberately does not try to be.

    NOTA screening tool

    Your institution keeps its existing screening system. CIP starts where that system's alert ends.

    NOTIts own sanctions or news database

    CIP uses official lists and licensed sources rather than trying to become a data provider.

    NOTA decision-maker

    It does not close alerts on its own or file regulatory reports. It prepares the case; your team decides.

    NOTA fixed 0–100 risk score engine

    The investigation follows your institution's own policies, and a human makes the final decision.

    NOTA cast of AI personas for show

    A small number of functional investigation roles, designed around how an investigation actually runs.

    About VrenceS Technologies

    A technology company building intelligent infrastructure for financial compliance.

    Our mission

    Make complex compliance investigation more structured, evidence-backed and efficient, while keeping humans firmly in control.

    Why the investigation layer

    Financial institutions already invest in screening. The harder, slower part comes after the alert: working out whether a flagged record is really the customer, reading the sources, and writing down why.

    That is the work we build for. CIP sits between the screening tool and the compliance analyst, prepares the investigation, and leaves the decision where it belongs.

    We are starting with compliance teams at Indian banks, NBFCs and fintechs.
